# Brag Plan: Pull

## What is this app?
Pull is an Android download manager that splits every file into parallel segments, with torrents, video-site links and a built-in ad-blocking browser.

## The angle
Release video for 0.1.1-alpha.4. It opens on the real splash animation (the comet head shoots in, three trails stretch out), then shows the product doing its job. No speed claims against other managers: the README says there are no benchmark numbers yet.

## Hook (first 3s)
The splash: comet head shoots in along the diagonal, overshoots, three trails spring out behind it. Then the wordmark "Pull" and "Fast download manager" (the app's own drawer subtitle).

## Key moments
- Segment bar splits: a finished worker takes half of the biggest remaining section.
- Downloads screen card filling up.
- Browser Shield panel: Ad blocker, Popup blocker, Cosmetic filtering toggle on one by one.
- Torrents, video sites, resume.

## Outro
Comet logo on brand green. "No accounts. No telemetry."

## User flow worth showing
Paste a link -> card shows segments filling -> pause, kill app, resume. Browser: open page -> Shield on.

## Tone
- Preset: app-store
- Creative direction: calm Material 3 product film
- Interpretation: clean slides, soft springs, consistent light SFX layer, no jokes.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 25s

## Visual identity (from the app)
- Background: #F8FAF0
- Accent: #386A20 (container #B8F397, on-container #1F5106)
- Text: #191D16
- Display/body font: Roboto Flex in the app; system sans-serif in the video
- Strongest visual: the comet mark and its splash animation

## Share copy (draft)
Pull 0.1.1-alpha.4: a download manager that splits every file into parallel segments.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed, light SFX layer
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-11 (114.84 BPM)
- Music treatment: fade in at 0, fade out over the last 1.5s, volume 0.3
- Music cue guidance: preset read. Strong cues 3.70, 8.96, 12.65, 17.91, 22.65. Beat grid ~0.53s.
- Audio-reactive treatment: none (skipped to keep the mark's motion faithful to the real splash)
- SFX posture: sparse, motion-matched
- Restraint rule: nothing over 0.75 volume

## Storyboard
1. Splash — 0-3.6s — comet animation, wordmark, subtitle.
2. Hook — 3.6-8.0s — "Split every file into parallel segments." plus a segment bar filling.
3. Engine — 8.0-12.4s — Downloads screen card, one segment splits in two. Caption from README.
4. Browser — 12.4-16.6s — Shield panel, three toggles flip on one by one (sequential, held after).
5. Everything else — 16.6-19.6s — three cards: Torrents and magnet links, Video sites, Resume.
6. Outro — 19.6-23s — logo, "Pull", "No accounts. No telemetry."

Music mood: warm upbeat. Audio summary: soft bed, drops on reveals, switch clicks on toggles, one bell on the logo.
